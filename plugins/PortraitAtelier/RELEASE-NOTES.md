## 0.1.5.1

- Background, frame and accent changes can each be enabled or disabled independently, including in settings backups.

- Expanded to 30 featured looks with eight additional community-inspired lighting/composition styles, including Crimson Spotlight, Azure Contour, Shadow Outline and Gallery Close-up. New styles also control directional-light angles.

- Owned designs load automatically from the open editor. Generate captures the current portrait if no base is loaded, and uses random owned designs when no preferences are set.

- Added the installed version in the bottom-right corner.
- Added 12 featured looks inspired by community portrait compositions, with matching owned-pose and expression preferences. New looks are included in the refreshable catalog.
