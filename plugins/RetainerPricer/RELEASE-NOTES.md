## 0.5.2

- Submarines: the trip to the workshop no longer stops at the loading screen, and a lost submersible selection after finalizing is retried.
- A sequence now ends by selling your whitelisted items to the NPC and reports the gil made. Turn it off in Automation if you prefer.
- Salvaged and Extravagant Salvaged accessories are now on the retainer sale whitelist by default; remove any you want to keep.
- Redeploy submarines also appears at the top beside the multi-character button while Show Submarines is enabled.
